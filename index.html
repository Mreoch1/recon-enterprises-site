About

Recon Enterprises works exclusively with general contractors and developers on multi-family renovation work. We started small, grew into larger work, and now run multiple projects at once across several states.

We’re selective about the work we take. Has nothing to do with being picky. A job done right beats ten done half right.

Same GCs keep calling us back. That’s the measure that matters to us.

Every project gets pre-construction planning, daily coordination, and a clean closeout. One point of contact, clear answers, no runaround.
