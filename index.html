About

Recon Enterprises partners with general contractors and developers on multi-family renovation work. We started small, moved into larger projects, and now manage multiple concurrent jobs across several states.

We are selective about the work we take. The same GCs keep calling us back, and that is the measure that matters.

Every project gets pre-construction planning, daily coordination, and a clean closeout. One point of contact, clear answers, no runaround.

Services

Exterior
