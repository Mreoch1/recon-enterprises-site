About

Recon Enterprises is a women-owned general contractor specializing in multi-family renovation and rehabilitation. We work exclusively with GCs, developers, and property owners on large-scale affordable housing projects.

We have extensive experience with affordable housing programs including MSHDA, HUD, and Section 3. Our team is experienced with the requirements and regulations that come with publicly funded projects.

We started small, moved into larger work, and now manage multiple concurrent jobs across several states. We are selective about the work we take. The same GCs keep calling us back.

Every project gets pre-construction planning, daily coordination, and a clean closeout. One point of contact, clear answers, no runaround.

Services

Exterior: Roofing, siding, windows, doors, concrete, pavement. The full building envelope.

Interior: Kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, paint, trim. Unit turns and common areas.

Full Rehab: Structural, MEP, elevator modernization, amenity spaces. We coordinate the whole scope.

Trade Management: A vetted network of subcontractors. One point of contact for scheduling, COIs, and on-site coordination.

Project Software: Procore • Building Connected • Smartsheet
