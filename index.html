About Us

Recon Enterprises, Inc. was established as a women-owned construction company serving the affordable housing industry. Under the leadership of Managing Director Michael Reoch, we have grown from a single project in 2020 to managing multiple simultaneous $4M+ renovation programs across Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania, and Maryland.

We partner exclusively with established general contractors and developers who share our commitment to quality — including Rose Community Builders and Ginosko Construction — delivering full-scope rehabilitation, unit turns, and building upgrades that stand the test of time.

Our model is built on relationships, not volume. We are selective about the work we take on because we believe that the best projects come from trust, clear communication, and a shared commitment to doing the job right.

Recon Enterprises manages every phase of construction — from pre-construction planning through closeout — with a dedicated team of experienced project managers, superintendents, and trade partners.

Our Approach

We believe that how you build matters as much as what you build. Our approach is rooted in respect — for the project, the residents, and the relationship.

Resident-First Construction

We minimize disruption to residents through phased scheduling, clear communication, and coordinated trade sequencing. Our team understands that these are people’s homes, not just job sites.

Reliability Over Lowest Bid

We do not chase volume. We take on work we can execute well, communicate honestly about timelines and budgets, and show up every day until the job is done.

Quality Materials & Workmanship

We use products and materials that enhance resident wellbeing and building performance. From energy-efficient windows to durable flooring and finishes, we build to last.

End-to-End Accountability

From pre-construction budgeting and scheduling through final punch list and closeout, one team owns the outcome. No handoffs, no dropped balls, no excuses.
