About

Recon Enterprises works exclusively with established general contractors and developers on multi-family renovation work — full-building rehabs, unit turns, exterior and interior upgrades. We handle projects across Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania, and Maryland.

Our team has been doing this for a while. We started small, grew into larger work, and now manage multiple renovation projects at once without missing a beat. It’s not complicated — we show up, do the job, and keep the client updated the whole way.

We’re selective about the work we take. Has nothing to do with being picky — we just believe a job done right beats ten jobs done half right. That’s why the same GCs keep calling us back.

Every project gets the same treatment. Pre-construction planning, daily coordination, clean closeout. One point of contact, clear answers, no runaround.

What We Do

Full-scope renovation capabilities. We self-perform most of the work and manage the rest through trades we already know and trust.

Exterior Work

Roofing, siding, windows, doors, concrete, pavement. We handle the building envelope from top to bottom.

Interior Finishes

Kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, paint, trim. Unit turns and common area upgrades. New or refresh.

Full-Building Rehab

Structural repairs, MEP upgrades, elevator modernization, amenity spaces. We coordinate the whole scope so you don’t have to.
