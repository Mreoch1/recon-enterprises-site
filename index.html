About

Built on Relationships, Not Bids

Recon Enterprises is a Michigan-based general contractor specializing in large-scale multi-family renovation and rehabilitation. We manage projects from pre-construction through closeout — partnering with GCs, developers, and property owners who value reliability over the lowest bid.

Since 2020, we’ve grown from one renovation project per year to managing three simultaneous $4M+ programs with Rose Community Builders and Ginosko Construction. Our model is simple: show up, execute, get paid, repeat.
